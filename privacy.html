Privacy Policy

Forevermore Messaging Program · Last updated 30 August 2026

Who this covers

Forevermore is a personal, self-hosted notification system operated by a sole proprietor. It sends automated SMS alerts to a single recipient: the operator of the system, on a mobile number he owns.

What information is collected

One mobile telephone number, entered by the system operator into a configuration file on hardware he owns. No information is collected from any other person. There is no sign-up form, no subscriber list, and no data collected from visitors to this page.

Message delivery metadata (timestamps, delivery status, and error codes) is generated by our telecommunications provider, Twilio, in the ordinary course of delivering messages, and is retained in that provider's account records.

How the information is used

The telephone number is used solely to deliver operational alerts to the system operator, such as notification that a background service has stopped or that a calendar event is approaching.

Information is not shared or sold

No information collected by this program is shared with third parties, sold, rented, or used for marketing purposes. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. The only third party involved is Twilio, which transmits the messages and cannot use the information for its own marketing.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies and depends on system events. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance. The recipient may also revoke consent by removing the number from the system configuration.
